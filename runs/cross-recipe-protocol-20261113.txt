## Why their multi-user "code" looks 2x ours — measurement artifact
clamp_range with thinking off EOSes at ~59 tokens: 8 streams = 472 tokens
total (vs 3200 for structured/prose which hit the 400 cap). At 472 tokens
the wall is PREFILL-shaped, not decode-shaped: 8 tiny prompts enter our
864-token mixed scheduler one chunk each (~150 ms/step class) = ~1.2 s of
the 1.4 s wall. Their 4096-chunk scheduler batches all 8 prefills into one
~280-token chunk (~40 ms). Not a decode regression: our code SOLO is +11%
and the 8-user structured/prose (true decode-bound classes) are at parity
with 2 FEWER pipeline hops' worth of cards doing more work per stage.

## Reading
