## Why no win here (and +2-3% on the peer)
Their gain rides 4096-token prefill chunks on PP2: many row tiles per
request per step make the stock request-x-tiles grid waste CTAs. Our chunk
is 864 on PP3: ~14 tiles per solo step (sparse grid either way — for a
single request compact == stock and the kernel falls back), and the QSA
indexer is a thin slice of our step. The ragged 4-user probe stresses
admission/queueing more than the indexer grid.

## Verdict
Not taken. No regression measured, but no measurable gain either — an
open-PR kernel diff in prod with zero return is pure rebase surface.
Re-visit if we ever move to larger prefill chunks or upstream merges it
(the base-jump rebase brings it free). Branch port-qsa-65470 kept.
