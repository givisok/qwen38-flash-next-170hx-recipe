User flagged the v0.33.0 release note. The PR is OLD NEWS for us: merged
2026-10-15, recorded as merged in the 11-03 dflash-wave sweep. Checked
against our fork rather than the headline:

- WHAT IT FIXED: max_num_new_slots_for_drafting() charged DFlash K and
  DSpark K-1 EXTRA scheduler slots per request for draft queries that run
  in a separate pass; the fix zeroes them and enforces draft-buffer
  capacity as a concurrency cap instead.
- OUR PROD (method=mtp): the same function in our base (config/
  speculative.py:1893, pre-fix table) returns 0 for mtp — mtp is not
  dflash, not parallel_drafting, not a draft_model. MTP reserved nothing
  before the PR and nothing after: NOTHING TO RECLAIM. Decode target
  batch = 16 reqs x (K+1)=5 -> 80 tokens of the 8192 budget, and those
  are real verification tokens, not reservations.
- SCALE CHECK for the dflash k5 shelf: even on our base's pre-fix code,
  K=5 x 16 seqs = 80 slots of 8192 = 1%. Their +147% needed 52-64
  concurrent requests squeezing the token budget into prefill slivers.
  Our 16-seq geometry cannot enter that regime; the fix rides free with
  the base-jump rebase if we ever revive dflash (alongside #64059).
- NICE CONFIRMATION: their failure chain "prefill slivers -> mixed
  prefill/decode steps -> miss the FULL decode graph" is exactly our
  measured storm property (4-5 tok/s inside chunks). On their box the
  slivers came from the bad reservation; ours come from the deliberate
  864 chunk (campaign-proven optimum). Same physics, different cause.
