## Verdict
The mechanism works on our stack (gates open, graph captured, mixed steps
dispatch into it) and changes nothing where it hurts: our mixed steps are
GPU-BOUND (864 tokens x layers + int8 hops fill the step by themselves), so
removing host-side overhead has nothing to remove — the author's 99->55 ms
mixed-step host time came from small host-bound agentic sends on A100. The
port's only clear effects here are negative: a per-step staging tax on every
build() (solo -6..-11%) and 1.2% of pool for the 944 graph. REVERTED.

Rollback verified: fork back to dflash2-pool @ f40a6d016d, 12 binds removed,
944 removed from the yml; gates re-run green (depth 4/4 at 100%, solo 137.1,
pool 5,282,636 / 5.04x). Branch port-gdn-61952 kept on the fork.

Revisit triggers: upstream merges the series AND our profile turns host-bound
(many small agent sends, small chunks), or a smaller-chunk config where mixed
steps approach the capture sizes anyway.
