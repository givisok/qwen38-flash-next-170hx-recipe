## Why it loses here (and won on the author's setup)
The PR's wins are at TP=8: the projections are sharded 8x narrower
(N=2048-2688), so the skinny GEMM's K-split pays off against cuBLASLt
split-K + splitKreduce. At our TP=1 the N dimension is 8x wider
(16384-248320) — plenty of output parallelism, cuBLASLt needs no split-K
and the skinny kernel's partials/reduction overhead just adds latency.
Phase A dropping even on replicated shapes says our card's cuBLASLt
baselines are also faster than the author's A100 relative to this kernel
(different SM count/clocks/L2 of the cut GA100).
The one M=1 keep is a token count we effectively never run: with MTP-4
the target model executes verify steps at M = 5 x seqs (5 solo), and the
draft forward is the MTP head's own linears, not these.
