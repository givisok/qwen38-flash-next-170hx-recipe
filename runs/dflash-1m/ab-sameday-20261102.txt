## Verdict
dflash k5 wins generation speed at every measured point today; the margin
peaks where this rig is used most (code +36% solo / +23-27% N=8) and holds
at full depth (+17% @1M). MTP-4's last argument — the YaRN wall — is gone.
Sensible next step if switching prod: dflash yml gmu 0.94 -> 0.95.
