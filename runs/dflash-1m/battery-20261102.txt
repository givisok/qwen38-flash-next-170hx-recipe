## Verdict
The YaRN-boundary wall was the drafter's cos/sin table running out at row
262,144 (rope_type default -> table sized by max_position_embeddings; the
speculator feeds real target positions to 1M; Triton reads past the end
silently). Extending the table to 1,048,576 rows with UNCHANGED frequencies
restores acceptance to baseline at every depth (1.79 acc/draft @512k/1M,
vs 1.62 @250k control) — rope angles below 262,144 are bit-identical, so
nothing in the working range changed (solo/battery/acceptance gates all
match). Residual yarn-space distribution shift on captured target states:
not measurable. dflash k5 is now a full-1M lane and out-decodes MTP-4 prod
at depth (141.7 vs 134 tok/s @1M probe protocol). Production kept on MTP-4
this run; switching is a separate decision.
